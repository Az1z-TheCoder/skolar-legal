PRIVACY POLICY: Skolår

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what information Skolår ("the app", "we") collects, how
it is used, and who it is shared with. It is written to describe the app's
actual behavior, not boilerplate.

Summary
-------
- No account, sign-up, or login. We never collect your name, email, or any
  other identifier.
- Your subject choices, practice history, streaks, planner tasks and daily
  usage counters are stored only on your device.
- Where AI marking is enabled in your build, the text of your typed answer
  (plus the relevant question and mark scheme) is sent to our grading
  server, which
  forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback. It is not
  linked to your identity, because we don't have one to link it to.
- Purchases (Exam Pass, once it is on sale) are processed by Apple or Google
  and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
- We show ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, personalized ads only run if you
  grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission; otherwise you see
  non-personalized ads.
- We do not use any analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDK.

1. Information we do NOT collect
---------------------------------
Skolår has no account system. There is no sign-up, login, password, email
address, or any other form of registration anywhere in the app. We have no
way to identify you as an individual, and we do not attempt to.

2. Information stored on your device
-------------------------------------
The app stores the following locally on your device (using the app's local
storage), and nowhere else:

  - the subjects you've selected to study, your chosen accent colors, and a
    rough graduating cohort year (e.g. "27" for the class of 2027). Not a
    birthdate or exact age, just the exam session you're working toward
  - your practice history: which questions you've answered and whether you
    got them right, which powers your streaks, accuracy statistics and
    weak-topic breakdowns
  - planner tasks you create (quizzes, tests, coursework and IA deadlines)
  - daily usage counters for the free tier (how many questions and
    flashcards you've used today, and rewarded-ad top-ups)
  - your app preferences (notification toggles, question-type and display
    choices) and small internal counters the app needs to behave sensibly,
    such as when an ad was last shown and when your streak was last
    celebrated

This information stays on your device. It is never transmitted to us, and
it is deleted if you uninstall the app. The one exception is when you
choose to share it yourself: the streak card, the Heads Up score and the
message to a parent all put figures you can see on screen into the share
sheet, and where that goes is your choice.

3. Purchases and subscriptions
--------------------------------
Exam Pass is not on sale yet, and until it is the app makes no purchases
and does not contact RevenueCat. This section applies from the day Exam
Pass is offered in the app.

Skolår Exam Pass (monthly, annual, or lifetime) is sold through Apple's App
Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we
never receive your card number, billing address, or store account identity.

To know whether your device has an active Exam Pass, the app uses
RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, as our processor. RevenueCat
receives purchase receipts from the store and a randomly generated,
anonymous app-installation identifier (not your name or email, because we
don't have those), plus basic device information needed to validate the
purchase.
RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. AI-assisted marking (extended-response grading)
----------------------------------------------------
Extended-response practice questions offer an optional "AI mark" feature. If
you choose to use it, the text of your answer is sent to a grading server we
operate, along with the relevant question text, mark scheme, and model
answer (no other data about you is included: not your profile, not your
subject list, not any identifier). Our server forwards this to Anthropic's
Claude API (a third-party AI provider) solely to generate a mark and written
feedback, which is returned to the app and shown to you.

  - Anthropic acts as our sub-processor for this feature only. Anthropic
    processes this content to generate the response and, per Anthropic's
    commercial API terms, does not use API content to train its models by
    default.
  - Answer text sent to the grading server is not linked to any account,
    because no account exists.
  - Our grading server does not log the contents of your answer or the
    question data; it only logs high-level operational events (e.g. that a
    request failed) for debugging the service itself.
  - If AI marking is not configured or unavailable, the app falls back to
    self-marking against the on-device mark scheme, and no answer text
    leaves your device.

5. Advertising
---------------
The free tier of Skolår shows ads through Google AdMob. This includes small
banners, occasional full-screen ads between activities, and rewarded ads
you can choose to watch in exchange for extra daily questions, flashcards,
or a streak freeze. Exam Pass removes advertising entirely.

  - On iOS, we ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before
    showing you personalized ads. If you decline, or haven't yet responded,
    you are shown non-personalized ads.
  - AdMob and its ad-serving partners may collect device-level information
    (such as device identifiers and coarse ad-interaction data) as
    permitted by your ATT choice, under Google's own privacy policy:
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - We do not control what AdMob's ad partners do with this data beyond the
    settings and permissions described above.

The app may periodically fetch a small remote configuration file that tunes
how often ads may appear. This request carries no personal data.

6. Analytics and tracking
---------------------------
The app does not include any analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDK
(no Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Sentry, Segment, or similar). We do not
track how you use the app beyond what's described above.

7. Other on-device permissions
--------------------------------
  - Calendar: optionally, to add your own revision and coursework deadlines
    to a calendar the app creates on your device. Calendar data stays in
    your device's calendar system and is never sent to us. If that
    calendar sits on an account that syncs, such as iCloud or Google, it
    syncs wherever that account syncs.
  - Motion: used only inside Heads Up mode to detect tilting the phone.
    Motion data is processed on the device in real time and never stored or
    transmitted.
  - Notifications: streak reminders and study nudges are scheduled locally
    on your device. There is no push-notification server.

8. Children's privacy
------------------------
Skolår is built for International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme students,
who are typically 16 years or older. The app is not directed at, and is not
knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13.

The app asks you for no directly identifying information. It has no
accounts, and we never see your name, email address or payment details. It
does allow Google's advertising SDK to collect a device advertising
identifier, and that identifier is personal data under GDPR and personal
information under COPPA, so we say so plainly here rather than claiming
that nothing at all is collected. On iOS that collection only happens if
you agree to the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, or never
answer, ads are non-personalised.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way not described
here, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

9. Data retention and deletion
---------------------------------
Because we don't operate accounts or store your data on our servers, there
is nothing tied to you for us to retain. On-device data is removed
automatically when you delete the app. On Android you can also clear the
app's storage in your device settings. On iOS there is no per-app storage
control, so deleting the app is the way to remove everything. Purchase records live with Apple or
Google and follow their retention rules.

10. Changes to this policy
-----------------------------
If our data practices change, we'll update this policy and the "Last
updated" date above.
