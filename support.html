SUPPORT: Skolår

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Skolår is IB revision practice: exam-style questions, flashcards and a
planner, across 18 Diploma Programme courses.

Contact
-------
Email: alsahaliabdulaziz@gmail.com

Skolår is built and supported by one person. Expect a reply within three
working days. Please write in English.

If you are reporting a problem, it helps a lot to include:
- your device and iOS version
- what you did, what happened, and what you expected
- the subject and topic, if the problem is with a question


Report a wrong question
-----------------------
This is the most useful message you can send us.

If a question has a wrong answer key, an explanation that does not match the
answer, an option that is also correct, or content outside your syllabus,
email us the subject, the topic, and the first line of the question. That is
enough for us to find it. Say what you think is wrong. Every report is read
and checked against the IB subject guide.

We would rather hear about a question that turns out to be correct than
leave a wrong one in the bank.


The free daily allowance
------------------------
The free tier gives you a set number of practice questions and flashcards
each day. The counter resets at midnight in your device's own time zone.

The home screen shows how many you have left. Watching a rewarded ad adds a
top-up on top of that day's allowance. Exam Pass removes the limit.


Purchases and subscriptions
---------------------------
Purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your payment
details.

To restore a purchase, open Settings inside the app and tap Restore
Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

To view, change or cancel a subscription, use Apple's own screen: open the
iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does
not cancel a subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We
cannot issue a refund on Apple's behalf.


Your data
---------
Skolår has no account and no login. Your practice history, streaks, planner
and daily counters are stored on your device only.

To delete everything, delete the app. That removes all local data. There is
no server-side profile to erase, because we never created one.

Where AI marking is enabled, the text of a typed answer is sent for grading
and is not linked to any identity. See the Privacy Policy for detail.


Streaks and notifications
-------------------------
A streak counts calendar days on which you studied, in your device's time
zone. Answering a question counts, and so do turning flashcards and playing
a round of Heads Up. Exam Pass includes streak freezes, which protect a day
you missed so the streak carries on.

Reminders are optional. If you declined the notification prompt and want
reminders later, enable notifications for Skolår in the iOS Settings app.


Accessibility
-------------
Skolår follows the system text size. If any screen is hard to read or a
control is hard to reach, please email us and say which screen. We treat
that as a bug.
